The current line of work

One question runs through everything below: what has to be true before an autonomous system can be put in front of real consequences? Not whether it performs well on a benchmark — whether its failures are bounded, whether anyone can tell what it did, and whether a person still has time to intervene when it matters.

These are analytical frameworks derived from published engineering practice and regulatory text, not empirical studies. Where a claim rests on external evidence, the source is cited in the article.
